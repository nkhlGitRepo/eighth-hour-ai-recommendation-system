Our Story

Eighth Hour started with a familiar problem: a founder who had spent decades building businesses kept running into the same daily snag — after every packed schedule, early flight, and late meeting, the hardest decision of the day was still what to wear.

Conversations with other working women echoed the same frustration: busy careers, changing bodies, and no patience for endless browsing. Not a desire to shop more, but a wardrobe they could simply trust.

That idea became Eighth Hour — clothing built to remove the noise. Well-cut pieces in natural fabrics, designed to be easy to reach for, easy to wear, and easy to live in, whether the day runs from a boardroom to an airport to dinner with friends.

Every piece is made with intention: fabrics that breathe, shapes that flatter without fuss, and finishes meant to hold up over years, not seasons.
