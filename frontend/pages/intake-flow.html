What makes a photo work

  • Your whole body in frame, head to feet. This is by far the most common reason a photo can't be used.
  • Stand square to the camera — shoulders and hips level, not turned or leaning.
  • Feet close together, arms slightly away from your sides.
  • Flat shoes or bare feet, on level ground.
  • Reasonably fitted clothing, even lighting, just you in the shot.

A phone propped up a few steps away, camera at about waist height, works better than a photo taken from above or at an angle.

We couldn't measure that photo

Your saved measurements haven't been changed. Most of the time the body wasn't fully in frame — it happens often, and it's easy to fix.

  • Check your whole body is in frame, head to feet — the single most common cause
  • Stand square to the camera, shoulders and hips level
  • Feet close together, flat shoes or bare feet
  • Avoid sitting, posing, leaning or turning to the side
  • Even lighting, fitted clothing, just you in the shot
